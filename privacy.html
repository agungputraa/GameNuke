Privacy Policy

How technical metrics and site data are handled across our portal, downloads, hosting, and application.

Overview

This policy outlines how the Game Nuke Official portal handles data when you browse our site, adjust language settings, or download releases. Android app policies depend on the build and permissions granted on your device.

Data used by the website

This site uses browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, and preferences cookies) strictly for essential functionality like remembering language preferences and maintaining download session context. We do not use this data to profile individual visitors.

Hosting providers and CDNs may log standard technical access data (IP address, user agent, requested URLs, timestamps) for operational security.

Device and app information

Within the app, telemetry tools read on-device metrics made available via Android system APIs or user-granted permissions (such as memory usage, thermal readings, frame statistics, and connection state). These metrics are processed locally to display live stats and run chosen utilities.

Game Nuke is not a cloud storage vault; never enter sensitive personal credentials or game account passwords into custom fields.

Third-party links and sponsor destinations

The portal may link to Google Play, GitHub release infrastructure, and official social accounts. Third-party sites govern their own data collection and privacy practices. The standalone download flow uses sponsor directlinks. When you click to download or continue, a new tab opens the next download page and the previous tab may navigate to a third-party advertiser. The sponsor destination may use cookies or collect technical data under its own policies. No purchase at the sponsor destination is required for the APK. The portal cannot control the content or tracking used by an external advertiser.

Advertising, cookies, and Google services

Sponsor destinations may use their own cookies or identifiers. If Google AdSense is enabled in the future, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads based on your visits. You can customize or opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

This source package does not include Google AdSense code or a consent platform. If Google advertising is enabled, the operator must configure the required consent platform and disclosures before serving ads in regions where consent is required.

Language preferences

The homepage can load Google Translate after you choose a non-English language. That request sends the usual connection information to Google; the selected language is saved in browser storage and the translation cookie. Public page typography uses device fonts and does not request Google Fonts.

Retention and control

Local browser storage can be cleared anytime in your browser settings. Support conversations via social platforms adhere to each respective platform's data retention policies.

Questions and Support

For privacy-related inquiries or data requests, please contact our support team at agungofficialdev@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
