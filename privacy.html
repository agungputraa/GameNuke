Privacy Policy

How technical metrics and site data are handled across our portal, downloads, hosting, and application.

Overview

This policy outlines how the Game Nuke Official portal handles data when you browse our site, adjust language settings, or download releases. Android app policies depend on the build and permissions granted on your device.

Data used by the website

This site uses browser storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, and preferences cookies) strictly for essential functionality like remembering language preferences and maintaining download session context. We do not use this data to profile individual visitors.

Hosting providers and CDNs may log standard technical access data (IP address, user agent, requested URLs, timestamps) for operational security.

Device and app information

Within the app, telemetry tools read on-device metrics made available via Android system APIs or user-granted permissions (such as memory usage, thermal readings, frame statistics, and connection state). These metrics are processed locally to display live stats and run chosen utilities.

Game Nuke is not a cloud storage vault; never enter sensitive personal credentials or game account passwords into custom fields.

Third-party links and sponsor destinations

The portal may link to Google Play, GitHub release infrastructure, and official social accounts. Third-party sites govern their own data collection and privacy practices. Any optional sponsor links are clearly disclosed during the download flow.

Advertising, cookies, and Google services

If Google AdSense or advertising services are active on this portal, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads based on your visits. You can customize or opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Required consent dialogs are displayed in relevant regulatory regions prior to ad personalization.

Retention and control

Local browser storage can be cleared anytime in your browser settings. Support conversations via social platforms adhere to each respective platform's data retention policies.

Questions

For privacy-related inquiries, reach out through our verified channels on the Contact page.
