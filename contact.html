Official support channels

For questions regarding installation, compatibility, or build versions, please use the official channels below. When reaching out, include your device model, Android version, Game Nuke version, and a brief summary of the issue for faster troubleshooting.

  • WhatsApp Community: Official WhatsApp Channel

  • Instagram: @agungeka_22
  • YouTube: @neoncoreofficialpro

How to report a technical issue

Describe the steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected to happen, and what occurred. If the issue involves overlays or elevated permissions, specify whether Accessibility, Shizuku, Wireless Debugging, or Display Over Other Apps is enabled.

Never share passwords, OTP codes, account credentials, or private keys with support.

Business and media enquiries

For partnership inquiries, media coverage, or technical reviews, reach out via our official social channels with clear details on your inquiry.

Security-related reports

If you identify a security concern, provide the build version, reproduction steps, and technical context. Please practice responsible disclosure to protect the community.
